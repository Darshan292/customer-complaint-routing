1. Knowledge Base Operating Model
This knowledge base is designed as a controlled, article-based operational library. Each article has a stable identifier, a defined audience,
symptoms, likely causes, safe procedures, validation criteria and escalation conditions. The same structure supports human search, semantic
retrieval and future GraphRAG or retrieval-augmented generation workflows.

1.1 How to use an article
      Identify the ticket symptom and confirm that the article applies before executing steps.
      Use the current ticket data, SLA state, category, subcategory, customer impact and existing audit trail as the primary context.
      Execute only steps authorized for the resolver role. Do not invent system facts or claim that a remediation succeeded without verification.
      Record material actions in the ticket timeline or internal comment stream where available.
      Escalate when the article says to escalate, when evidence contradicts the article, or when the ticket crosses an approval boundary.

1.3 Retrieval fields
Recommended metadata for indexing: article_id, title, domain, product_area, symptom, category, subcategory, priority, resolver_role,
keywords, prerequisites, procedure, validation, escalation, related_article_ids, effective_date, owner, version.

    RETRIEVAL PRINCIPLE

    operational condition, then use text similarity to rank within that filtered set.

2.1 Resolver assignment gates
       Correct operational team is a hard boundary for automatic routing.
       Resolver must be active and configured for automatic assignment.
       Normal automatic routing requires workforce eligibility, schedule alignment and capacity availability.
       Skill fit influences ranking; it does not replace a hard eligibility rule.
       Priority load and SLA exposure reduce the score of heavily burdened resolvers.
       On-call routing is an emergency fallback for defined priority conditions when normal workforce candidates are unavailable.
       Manual admin assignment is an operational override and must be auditable.

3. KB-ACC-001 - Account locked / unable to sign in
Use when a customer cannot access the application because the account appears locked or login attempts are rejected.

    Field                                                                              Value


    Article ID                                                                         KB-ACC-001


    Audience                                                                           Customer Support, Technical Support, Resolver


    Severity / Priority                                                                Medium to High


    Applies when                                                                       Customer reports locked account or repeated login rejection.


    Related articles                                                                   KB-SEC-001, KB-TECH-001




Resolution context
      Symptom: Customer reports locked account or repeated login rejection.
      Symptom: No evidence yet of malicious account takeover.
      Likely cause: Account lockout policy triggered.
      Likely cause: Incorrect credentials or stale browser/session state.
      Likely cause: Identity synchronization or authentication service issue.

Prerequisites and guardrails
      Confirm customer identity using approved verification controls.
      Do not request or store passwords, MFA secrets or recovery codes.
      Review ticket history for recent password or security changes.

Resolution procedure
1.     1. Confirm exact error text and approximate start time.
2.     2. Check whether other users in the same tenant are affected.
3.     3. Verify whether the identity provider/authentication dependency is operational.
4.     4. If the issue is a local lockout, use the authorized account-unlock workflow.
5.     5. Ask the customer to retry through a clean session or approved client path.
6.     6. If the error persists across users or tenants, move to technical incident analysis.

Validation and closure criteria
      Customer can authenticate successfully.
      No active lockout remains.
      Ticket contains verification evidence and final action taken.

Escalation
      Escalate to Cyber Security when takeover indicators appear.
      Escalate to Site Reliability when multiple users/tenants are simultaneously affected.
      Escalate to platform owner when identity synchronization remains inconsistent after approved remediation.

    OPERATOR NOTE
    Never bypass identity verification merely to meet SLA.

4. KB-ACC-002 - Password reset email not received
Use when password reset was requested but the verification/reset email does not arrive.

    Field                                                                              Value


    Article ID                                                                         KB-ACC-002


    Audience                                                                           Customer Support, Technical Support, Resolver


    Severity / Priority                                                                Medium


    Applies when                                                                       Customer can access email but no reset message is received.


    Related articles                                                                   KB-ACC-001, KB-OPS-002




Resolution context
      Symptom: Customer can access email but no reset message is received.
      Symptom: Reset request may succeed in the application but email is absent.
      Likely cause: Mailbox filtering or spam quarantine.
      Likely cause: Email delivery delay.
      Likely cause: Incorrect customer email or stale identity record.
      Likely cause: Notification provider outage.

Prerequisites and guardrails
      Confirm the destination email without exposing private account data.
      Check whether the customer has received any other system notifications.
      Do not disclose whether an account exists to an unverified party.

Resolution procedure
7.     1. Confirm request timestamp and channel.
8.     2. Ask customer to check spam/quarantine and corporate mail filters.
9.     3. Verify the application generated the reset request successfully.
10.    4. Retry only through the approved reset mechanism.
11.    5. If repeated attempts fail, inspect notification delivery telemetry or notification status.
12.    6. Escalate repeated delivery failures as a platform/notification incident.

Validation and closure criteria
      Customer receives a valid reset message.
      Customer can complete password reset.
      No duplicate or unsafe reset tokens remain active.

Escalation
      Escalate to notification/platform operations for repeated delivery failure.
      Escalate to security if the reset request appears unauthorized.

    OPERATOR NOTE
    Avoid telling the customer that an account does or does not exist based solely on email delivery behavior.

5. KB-SEC-001 - Suspicious login alert / suspected credential
compromise
Use when a customer reports foreign login alerts, suspicious sessions or possible credential compromise.

    Field                                                                           Value


    Article ID                                                                      KB-SEC-001


    Audience                                                                        Cyber Security, Fraud Investigation, Resolver


    Severity / Priority                                                             Critical


    Applies when                                                                    Unexpected login from an unfamiliar location.


    Related articles                                                                KB-SEC-002, KB-FIN-001




Resolution context
      Symptom: Unexpected login from an unfamiliar location.
      Symptom: Customer believes credentials were compromised.
      Likely cause: Credential theft.
      Likely cause: Session/token compromise.
      Likely cause: Compromised endpoint or browser.

Prerequisites and guardrails
      Treat as a potential security incident.
      Use the approved identity verification process.
      Do not ask for passwords, MFA codes or private recovery secrets.

Resolution procedure
13.    1. Capture the alert time, location and observed session information available to the customer.
14.    2. Determine whether the customer still controls the registered communication channel.
15.    3. Restrict or invalidate sessions using the approved security workflow when authorized.
16.    4. Force credential reset through the approved mechanism.
17.    5. Review recent account activity for unauthorized changes or transactions.
18.    6. Route financial-loss indicators to Fraud Investigation; escalate material or multi-account events to security incident handling.

Validation and closure criteria
      Unauthorized sessions are contained.
      Credential reset is completed through approved controls.
      Any financial or data-impact findings are captured and routed.

Escalation
      Immediate escalation for active compromise, privileged access, data exposure or financial loss.
      Escalate to Fraud Investigation for unauthorized payments.

    OPERATOR NOTE
    Speed matters, but evidence handling and identity verification remain mandatory.

    Field                                                                         Value


    Article ID                                                                    KB-SEC-002

    Severity / Priority                                                           Critical


    Applies when                                                                  Customer can view another user/customer record.


    Related articles                                                              KB-SEC-001, KB-OPS-004




Resolution context
      Symptom: Customer can view another user/customer record.
      Symptom: Screenshot or evidence indicates unauthorized data access.
      Likely cause: Authorization policy defect.
      Likely cause: Tenant/row-level data isolation failure.
      Likely cause: Cache or session contamination.
      Likely cause: Configuration or deployment regression.

Prerequisites and guardrails
      Treat as a potential privacy/security incident.
      Preserve evidence and avoid broad testing with real customer data.

      Incident owner confirms remediation.
      Required notifications and audit records are complete.

Escalation
      Immediate security/privacy escalation.
      Operations manager approval required for customer-impacting containment changes.

    OPERATOR NOTE
    This article is not a routine resolver troubleshooting procedure; follow incident-management controls when exposure is confirmed.

7. KB-FIN-001 - Duplicate charge / duplicate payment
Use when a customer reports being charged twice for the same purchase or subscription.

    Field                                                                           Value


    Article ID                                                                      KB-FIN-001


    Audience                                                                        Finance Operations, Resolver


    Severity / Priority                                                             High


    Applies when                                                                    Two or more transactions appear to represent the same intended
                                                                                    purchase.


    Related articles                                                                KB-FIN-002, KB-SEC-001




Resolution context
      Symptom: Two or more transactions appear to represent the same intended purchase.
      Symptom: Customer requests reversal or refund.
      Likely cause: Duplicate submission.
      Likely cause: Payment retry after timeout.
      Likely cause: Gateway duplication.
      Likely cause: Delayed authorization/capture creating apparent duplicates.

Prerequisites and guardrails
      Use transaction identifiers, not card secrets.
      Do not promise a refund before transaction status is verified.
      Follow finance approval limits for refunds/reversals.

Resolution procedure
25.    1. Capture transaction date, amount, currency and non-sensitive reference identifiers.
26.    2. Determine whether both transactions are settled, pending or reversed.
27.    3. Check for duplicate order/subscription records.
28.    4. If one transaction is only an authorization, explain the expected release path.
29.    5. If both are settled duplicates, initiate the approved refund/reversal workflow.
30.    6. Record the finance action and reference number in the ticket.

Validation and closure criteria
      Customer sees one valid charge or two clearly differentiated legitimate transactions.
      Refund/reversal reference is recorded when applicable.
      Customer communication matches the actual transaction state.

Escalation
      Escalate large-value or disputed transactions beyond resolver authority.
      Escalate suspected fraud to Fraud Investigation.

    OPERATOR NOTE
    Never paste full payment card numbers or authentication data into the ticket or comments.

8. KB-FIN-002 - Subscription paid but service not activated
Use when payment succeeded but the purchased entitlement is not active.

    Field                                                                           Value


    Article ID                                                                      KB-FIN-002


    Audience                                                                        Finance Operations, Technical Support


    Severity / Priority                                                             High


    Applies when                                                                    Payment is confirmed but expected service remains unavailable.


    Related articles                                                                KB-FIN-001, KB-TECH-002




Resolution context
      Symptom: Payment is confirmed but expected service remains unavailable.
      Symptom: Customer has a valid order/subscription reference.
      Likely cause: Entitlement provisioning delay.
      Likely cause: Payment-to-subscription synchronization failure.
      Likely cause: Incorrect account mapping.
      Likely cause: Activation job failure.

Prerequisites and guardrails
      Confirm payment using approved transaction/reference data.
      Verify the target account and purchased plan.
      Do not manually alter entitlement records outside approved operations.

Resolution procedure
31.    1. Verify transaction status and subscription/order identifier.
32.    2. Confirm the intended customer account and plan.

34.    4. If a provisioning job failed, route to the owning technical queue.
35.    5. If payment is valid but entitlement is absent, coordinate finance and technical teams.
36.    6. Communicate the next expected milestone to the customer.

Validation and closure criteria
      Service entitlement is active.
      Payment and subscription records reconcile.
      Customer confirms access or the platform confirms successful activation.

Escalation
      Escalate provisioning/systemic activation failures.
      Escalate payment disputes through Finance Operations.

    OPERATOR NOTE
    Do not solve a finance reconciliation problem by changing technical entitlements without matching transaction evidence.

14. KB-OPS-001 - Ticket is unassigned / no automatic resolver
Use when AI classification succeeds but no eligible resolver is available.

    Field                                                                           Value


    Article ID                                                                      KB-OPS-001


    Audience                                                                        Service Desk, Operations Admin


    Severity / Priority                                                             Operational


    Applies when                                                                    Ticket is OPEN and unassigned.


    Related articles                                                                KB-OPS-002, KB-OPS-003




Resolution context
      Symptom: Ticket is OPEN and unassigned.
      Symptom: Assignment analysis reports no eligible resolver.
      Likely cause: Outside working shift.
      Likely cause: Resolver at capacity.
      Likely cause: No qualified skill match.
      Likely cause: Team taxonomy mismatch.
      Likely cause: All normal workforce candidates unavailable.

Prerequisites and guardrails
      Do not bypass the operational team boundary.
      Use manual assignment only to an active resolver in the correct operational team unless an approved exception exists.

Resolution procedure
67.    1. Open assignment analysis and review all candidate explanations.
68.    2. Confirm the operational team resolved from the ticket classification.
69.    3. Review workforce status, shift, capacity and skill signals.
70.    4. If an eligible resolver exists, use auto-assignment.
71.    5. If no automatic candidate exists, use authorized manual assignment.
72.    6. Record the reason for the override.
73.    7. If no resolver exists in the team, escalate to workforce/queue administration.

      Assignment method and reason are captured.

15. KB-OPS-002 - SLA breached / SLA at risk
Use when a ticket is approaching or has exceeded its response or resolution SLA.

    Field                                                                          Value


    Article ID                                                                     KB-OPS-002


    Audience                                                                       Service Desk, Resolver, Operations Manager


    Severity / Priority                                                            High to Critical

    Related articles                                                               KB-OPS-001, KB-OPS-004




Resolution context
      Symptom: SLA state is AT_RISK or BREACHED.
      Symptom: Customer impact may be increasing because of delay.

      Likely cause: High workload on resolver.
      Likely cause: Customer dependency.
      Likely cause: Queue starvation.

    OPERATOR NOTE
